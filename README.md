##  What Makes This Different

### 1. Custom Glowing Cursor (Desktop)
Your cursor is replaced with a soft glowing orb that fades at the edges as you move across the hero section. The word **"LIGHT"** is hidden in the background — it only becomes visible as the glow passes over it. No library. No plugin. Pure JavaScript.

### 2. Day / Night Product Toggle
A toggle switch in the product section lets you flip between day and night mode. In **night mode**, every pendant lamp in the product cards actually **lights up** — the glow activates, the atmosphere shifts. Flip back to day and they turn off.

This isn't dark mode. It's a live product preview that shows the customer exactly how the lamp will look installed in their space — before they buy.
